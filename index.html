Donor Eligibility Guidelines

Learn about general safety standards for blood donors before submitting your request.

Donors must be between 18 and 65 years of age, weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs), and be in good general health at the time of donation.
For whole blood donations, the minimum interval is 56 days (8 weeks) for male donors and 84 days (12 weeks) for female donors to ensure iron stores are replenished.
Deferral conditions include: recent tattoo or piercing (6-month deferral), active flu or virus infection, travel to malaria-endemic zones (usually 12-month deferral), or major surgeries.
O Negative (O-) is the universal donor; their blood can be received by any blood type in emergencies. AB Positive (AB+) is the universal recipient; they can receive blood from any blood type.
